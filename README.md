# Tropical Fish & Coral: live demo

A WordPress + WooCommerce store theme for a marine livestock, dry goods and reef supplies shop.

**Try it:** https://playground.wordpress.net/?blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/azpilot2211/tropical-fish-coral-demo/main/blueprint.json

The link builds a fresh, private copy of the store inside your browser (about a minute the first time). Nothing you do there is saved or shared. In the demo, log in at `/wp-admin/` (you are signed in automatically) to see the importer, state shipping and category tools.

- `tropical-fish-coral.zip` is the theme
- `tfc-supplier-import.zip` is the plugin (supplier import, spreadsheet upload, shipping by state, photo finder)
- `demo-products.json` is a sample of 98 products with prices; `demo-setup.php` and `tfc-demo.php` load them
- Demo product photos load from the original suppliers' websites and belong to them.
